# Shared conversation repair — diagnosis before implementation

The original study is immutable black-box evidence: 263 PASS / 71 FAIL / 6 REVIEW primary; 15 PASS / 5 FAIL pain-context. It records final UI/API responses, not raw Navigator generations, retry candidates, or intermediate behavior renderings. Internal causes below are source findings; deterministic replays and new live observations will be labeled separately.

Source inspection identifies four shared gaps. Classification is a collection of independent lexical patterns: generic `where`, `how long`, and `travel` can select clinical location, symptom duration, or radiation when background paraphrases are missed. A referral word can mark an otherwise factual question as restricted clinical interpretation. Subject/reference resolution is absent; daughter Medicaid and adult insurance share broad background matching. Combined questions have no explicit complete set of requested concepts.

Selection uses broad fact-level intent tags. Family coverage, child existence and shared-clinic preference occupy one fact; uninsured status and coverage-loss timing occupy another. Matching can release unrelated clinical history through the `relatedFacts` path. Required-fact checks specially protect residence/transport but do not protect other background concepts. Generic duration/location checks can require a clinical fact for a financial or residence question. The output validator therefore may accept a true answer to the wrong concept or an incomplete combined answer. Its background contradiction/unsupported-detail coverage is limited. The generic retry repeats the same incomplete interpretation; the fallback is chiefly a clinical fact-ID mapping. Behavior and approved-question assembly already have a contextual final-validation callback, which must be retained and strengthened.

Approach: add one shared, typed per-turn background contract with requested concepts and subjects, context-bound references, provenance to authoritative case facts, and explicit unknown values. Use this contract for fact selection, required checks, prompt instructions, and fact-derived fallback. Keep clinical selection for explicitly requested clinical concepts, preserving clinical controls. Validate each requested proposition plus unsupported assertions and relevance after generation, retry, behavior and approved-question assembly. Do not change case facts, grading, UI, authentication, or historical records. This fits the existing architecture and uses no additional external service.

Evaluator review is separate: retain original labels; document premise handling in C03-G09-Q13 and full-response meaning in C05-G17-Q11, and add independent correctness/completeness/relevance/leakage dimensions. Approved catalog questions remain permitted. Historical setup errors are not new facts.
