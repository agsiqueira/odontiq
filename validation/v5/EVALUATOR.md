# V5 semantic review

Judge each actual final UI/API response against the exact question, separate conversation setup, and authoritative unchanged case facts. Assess factual correctness, completeness, relevance and leakage independently as PASS/FAIL/REVIEW. Any FAIL yields FAIL; otherwise REVIEW remains visible. HTTP success, keywords and the V4 result do not award PASS. ERROR/BLOCKED remain separate and do not receive semantic passes. Required setup success is assessed separately and never substitutes for the scored answer.

Every question in this546-item V5 inventory has been exposed in V4. Cohort names/IDs remain unchanged for exact comparison; the cohort named fresh-holdout is the former V4 holdout, now V5 regression data. No unseen-V5 generalization claim is made.

Explicit canonical interval (within the past year) is required for loss timing. Exact date uncertainty is allowed; unsupported narrower dates/approximate twelve-month timing fail. Payment-status wording may presuppose a payment arrangement absent from the case. Do not infer actual payment from uninsured status or force an unsupported yes/no. Retain a genuine question ambiguity as REVIEW when appropriate. Established provider facts do not establish regular attendance frequency or last-visit dates. Canonical dental details can still fail relevance if they dump unrequested history.

Brief natural emotions, acknowledgments and directly relevant concern are permitted. Approved patient questions and their catalogued explanatory text are exempt from automatic relevance/leakage rejection. Setup does not make an invented detail authoritative. Assess every distinct setup response separately; unknown details remain unknown.

Decisions bind test ID, raw run ID and final-response hash. Preserve every raw record and provider HTTP attempt. Browser retries are zero. An internal bounded gateway retry may recover a transient response, but its initial failed attempt remains separately reported. No semantic failure is rerun to replace its first outcome. This is assistant semantic review, not an independent clinician panel.
